## Data sources

- **HydroRIVERS dataset**

    Lehner, B., Grill G. (2013): Global river hydrography and network routing: baseline data and
new approaches to study the world’s large river systems. Hydrological Processes, 27(15):
2171–2186. Data is available at [www.hydrosheds.org](www.hydrosheds.org).

- **NaturalEarth dataset**

    [NaturalEarth data: "Admin 0 - Countries" 1:50m Cultural Vector](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/50m-cultural-vectors/): _De facto_ boundaries of countries in the world.
